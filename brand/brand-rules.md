# RSS Brand Rules

## Identity

Company: Razeen Secure Solution
Short brand: RSS

## Logo

Use the original RSS company logo unchanged.
- No recoloring
- No stretching
- No distortion
- No redesign
- No replacement logo
- No excessive sizing
- Preserve transparent background when supplied

## App and project logos

Every RSS app/project may have its own original logo.
- Use the original project/app logo for app identity: splash, registration, welcome, feature pages, main app identity, and project-specific branding.
- Do not substitute the RSS company logo for the project logo.
- Do not generate replacement logos.
- Preserve the project logo unchanged unless the project explicitly requests a different treatment.

## RSS company-branding placement

The original RSS company logo is reserved for designated company-branding areas:
1. Bottom of the RSS slide menu.
2. Bottom of Settings.
3. Bottom of About.
4. Bottom of Contact.
5. Bottom of Privacy Policy.
6. Bottom of Terms & Conditions.

A project may explicitly define additional company-branding placement for that project.

## Visual direction

Default: modern, premium, professional, lightweight Android UI.

Preferred brand direction: black + gold, adapted for accessibility and the active theme.

## Priority

Project-specific explicit branding instructions may override these defaults for that project.
