# Canonical RSS Logo Assets

The RSS Brand Kit contains three approved original logo assets.

## 1. Logo Only
**File:** `RSS Logo Only.png`

Use for:
- App icons and compact brand placements
- Registration/menu areas where the symbol alone is appropriate
- General RSS symbol use

## 2. Main Logo — Logo With Name
**File:** `RSS Logo with Name Transparent.png`

This is the **primary RSS company logo**.

Use for:
- **Android splash screens**
- Company branding
- Settings/about/company pages
- Documents, websites, presentations and marketing
- Places where the full company identity should be shown

### Splash-screen rule
Use **Main Logo — Logo With Name** on RSS app splash screens. Display it proportionally at an appropriate size with sufficient clear space. **Never oversize, zoom, stretch, crop, or distort the logo.**

## 3. Favicon
**File:** `RSS Logo Favicon.png`

Use for:
- Website favicon
- Browser/app web shortcut icons
- Other small-size web/icon contexts

## Logo Protection Rules

These three files are the approved original RSS assets.

- Do **not** recolor.
- Do **not** stretch or distort.
- Do **not** redesign or regenerate.
- Do **not** replace them with AI-generated alternatives.
- Do **not** crop the main logo in a way that changes its identity.
- Scale proportionally and keep appropriate clear space.
- Choose the asset according to its intended use above.

Project-specific original logos remain separate from these company-brand assets.
