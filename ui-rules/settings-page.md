# RSS Settings Page

Default structure:
- App/project identity at the top where appropriate
- Grouped settings sections
- Icon on the left
- Setting title/content on the right
- Clear section hierarchy
- Modern cards and separators
- Light/dark theme support
- RSS branding/company section near the bottom where appropriate

Common company section may include RSS logo, Razeen Secure Solution, About, Contact, Terms, Privacy and version information.

Do not force irrelevant common settings into a project.
